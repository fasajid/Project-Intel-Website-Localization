A timeline of progress

Sustainability through the ages

Every milestone adds momentum to the work still ahead.

1968

Intel Founded

Intel is founded to advance semiconductor innovation.

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore establish Intel after renaming NM Electronics, creating a company focused on semiconductor memory and future computing.

The company begins with a focus on semiconductor memory and future-focused computing.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel launches the world's first commercial microprocessor.

The 4004 microprocessor brings programmable logic to commercial products and helps launch the modern computing era.

The 4004 contains 2,300 transistors and helps make programmable electronics possible.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 processor establishes Intel's x86 architecture.

Intel's 8086 creates a durable architecture that supports decades of personal computers, servers, and software.

The x86 instruction set becomes a foundation for generations of personal computers.

1985

386 Processor

The 386 brings 32-bit performance to personal computers.

The 386 expands computing capability with 32-bit architecture, making more powerful software and multitasking accessible to PC users.

Its larger address space supports more capable software and richer multitasking.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches its highest annual operational greenhouse gas emissions.

The emissions peak becomes a baseline for Intel's investment in chemical abatement, renewable energy, and more efficient manufacturing.

This turning point makes emissions reduction a central part of manufacturing strategy.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE strategy and 2030 sustainability goals.

RISE stands for Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, and Enabling, connecting Intel's business growth with measurable environmental and social goals.

The goals connect climate action with water stewardship, waste reduction, and inclusion.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2040.

This commitment covers emissions from Intel's global operations and builds on years of work in energy efficiency and environmental management.

The commitment focuses on emissions from Intel's owned and operated facilities.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity worldwide.

Switching to renewable electricity helps Intel lower operational carbon emissions while supporting progress toward its long-term climate targets.

Renewable electricity becomes a major lever for reducing operational carbon impact.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel brings industry leaders together for its first Sustainability Summit.

The summit connects suppliers, government officials, and technology leaders to explore practical ways to make semiconductor manufacturing more sustainable.

Collaboration across the semiconductor ecosystem helps scale practical solutions.

2040

Net-Zero Operations

Intel targets net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across global operations.

The 2040 target represents Intel's long-term path toward lower-impact technology production through cleaner energy and efficient operations.

Progress depends on cleaner energy, efficient operations, and continued technology innovation.
